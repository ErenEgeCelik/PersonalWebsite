About

I'm a physics undergraduate at Middle East Technical University, expecting to graduate in 2028. I started at İzmir Institute of Technology with a 3.6 GPA and transferred to METU to be closer to the research I wanted to do.

Physics is where I learned to take a system apart until the assumptions are visible. Prediction markets are where I test whether I actually did. Over the last months I've been trading Polymarket with my own money and reverse-engineering the market maker on the other side of my fills — deriving a Brownian-probit fair value, an inventory-skew rule under CARA utility, and a Markov decision layer that eventually told me the strategy's ceiling was queue priority, not my model. I wrote that up too.

Before markets: a summer in Prof. Ali Bozbey's group at TOBB ETÜ, which built Turkey's first superconducting quantum computing hardware, and a stint in electronics manufacturing in Stuttgart doing SMD, AOI and instrument software. I like the whole stack — the physics, the electronics, and the code that logs it all at the millisecond.
